Log Workout

Record what you did so you can look back at your progress. Logging takes about a minute, and nothing is saved until you press Save Workout.

What do sets, reps, and weight mean?

A set is one round of an exercise. Reps is how many times you did it in that set. Weight is how heavy it was, in pounds. For exercises with no added weight, enter 0.
